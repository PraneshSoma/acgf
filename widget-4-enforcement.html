ACGF — Enforcement

The graduated enforcement ladder

Enforcement is not a binary switch. Each step has defined trigger conditions, duration limits, and appeal mechanisms. Click any step to see trigger criteria.

1
Advisory
Structured warning header sent to agent. No requests blocked.
Trigger: request rate <20% above certified baseline, duration <5 min. Single response header. No principal notification required. Auto-clears on next compliant request.
▾
2
Throttle
Request rate capped below normal ceiling. Requests still served at reduced volume.
Trigger: sustained drift >20% above baseline for >5 min, or risk score >0.4. Duration: 15–60 min auto-expiry. Principal notification issued. Appeal via certified behavioral declaration.
▾
3
Queue
Non-urgent requests deferred to lower-priority processing. Time-sensitive requests still honored.
Trigger: risk score >0.6, or platform load >85% combined with Tier 3 agent activity. Duration: until platform load normalizes. Settlement patterns activate for in-flight transactions.
▾
4
Suspend
Access temporarily suspended. Structured notice issued to accountable principal.
Trigger: risk score >0.8, confirmed behavioral profile violation, or unresolved escalation from Throttle/Queue. Duration: pending review, max 72 hr. Full settlement pattern execution required.
▾
5
Revoke
Certification revoked. All access terminated. Revocation published to certification authority.
Trigger: confirmed repeated violations, fraudulent certification attestation, or unresolved Suspend after 72 hr. Permanent — full re-certification required. Revocation record shared across NCOL federation.
▾
